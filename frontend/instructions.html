Automatic submission

  • When the time runs out, your answers are submitted for you.
  • Leaving fullscreen starts a 15-second countdown. Return to fullscreen to cancel it.
  • You get 3 warnings for switching tabs or windows. The next switch submits your test.
  • Do not refresh or close the page. Your answers are saved as you go.

Moving around

  • Use the section tabs, the question numbers, or Previous / Next.
  • “Review all answers” shows what is answered and what is marked for review.
  • Make the question text larger with the A+ button.
  • On a keyboard: A–E choose an option, ← → move between questions, F marks for review.

You’ll be asked to switch to fullscreen first. The timer starts after that.
